## The Power of Absolute Control
**AbsoluteWindows** is not just a tool; it's your system's personal commander. Designed for elite users, it condenses complex administrative tasks into a single, high-speed accessible interface. No more digging through Windows settings—just pure, raw control at your fingertips.

## Adaptive Intelligence
The core of AbsoluteWindows is its **State-Aware Engine**. The menu intelligently monitors your PC and changes options dynamically based on what you actually need:

> ### 🌐 Network Connectivity
> * **If Online:** Shows **"Disconnect Internet"**. One click instantly severs the connection for privacy or maintenance.
> * **If Offline:** Instantly transforms to **"Connect Internet"**, allowing you to restore your link in a split second.

> ### 🛡️ Security & UAC
> * **If Active (High Protection):** Shows **"Disable User Account Control"**. Quickly drops UAC to 0% for a seamless, prompt-free admin experience.
> * **If Silent (Unprotected):** Shows **"Enable User Account Control"**. Instantly restores the secure 40% (Default) level to guard your system.

## How to Invoke
Access the Command Center from anywhere in Windows with the global master shortcut:

<p align="center">
  <code>Alt + Windows + W</code>
</p>

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** AbsoluteWindows uses **Auditory Confirmation**. Listen for the High Tone for success and the Low Tone for failure/cancellation.

## Full Capability List
* **Manage Connections:** Toggle Internet adapters and retrieve Wi-Fi keys.
* **System Maintenance:** Manage Services, Startup apps, and Drive optimization (TRIM/Defrag).
* **Deep Cleaning:** Purge System files, Prefetch, Recent History, Temp files, and Windows Run logs.
* **Power Tools:** Clear RAM cache, empty Recycle Bin, and generate HTML Battery Health reports.
